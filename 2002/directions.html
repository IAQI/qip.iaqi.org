The Hampton Inn Hotel in White Plains/Tarrytown/Elmsford, New York, is holding rooms for workshop participants.

After your application for registration has been accepted, please call the Hotel yourself at 1-914-592-5680 to make reservations.

Directions

From Westchester County Airport to The Hampton Inn Hotel

Take 684 South to
287 West
Get off at exit 1
Make a left at the ramp
The Hampton Inn hotel is on the right hand side on the hill

From Laguardia Airport to The Hampton Inn Hotel

1. Take the Grand Central Parkway to
2. The Whitesone Expressway
3. Follow signs for the White Stone Bridge, and go over the White Stone Bridge
4. Follow signs for The Hutchinson River Parkway North
5. Take the Hutchinson River Parkway to
6. exit 1, which is Elmsford (Route 119)
7. Make a left at the end of the ramp and the Hampton Inn is1/8 of a mile on the right

From John F. Kennedy Airport to The Hampton Inn Hotel

1. Take The Van Wyck Expressway to
2. The Whitestone Expressway
3. Follow signs for the White Stone Bridge and go over the WhiteStone Bridge
4. Follow signs for the Hutchinson River Parkway North
5. Take the Hutchinson River Parkway to
6. Exit 1, which is Elmsford (Route 119)
7. Make a left at the end of the ramp and the Hampton Inn is 1/8 of a mile on the right

From The Hampton Inn Hotel to IBM

1. Start out going south east on main street by turning right
2. Take the Route 100A ramp
3. Turn left onto Knollwood road
4. Turn left to take the I-287 west ramp
5. Merge onto I-287 west
6. Take the Sprain Brook Parkway exit, exit #13 towards New York City/Taconic Parkway
7. Merge onto Sprain Brook Parkway North
8. Sprain Brook Parkway North becomes Taconic State Parkway North
9. Continue North to Route 134 exit
10. Turn right and proceed east on Route 134 several hundred yards
11. IBM is on the right

From IBM to The Hampton Inn Hotel

1. Turn left out of IBM
2. Turn left onto Taconic State Parkway South
3. Taconic State Parkway South will turn into the Sprain Brook Parkway South
4. Take the I-287 east exit on the left towards White Plains
5. Take the Route 100A exit, exit #4 towards Hartsdale
6. Turn right onto Knollwood Road
7. Take the Route 119 West Ramp
