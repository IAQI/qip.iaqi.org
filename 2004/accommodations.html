The Waterloo Inn

The Waterloo Inn is the recommended hotel for the QIP conference, as several events will be held there. A block of rooms has been held for conference attendees. Transportation will be provided to and from CIGI, the conference location, each day. (The details of the shuttle service are provided on the Travel Information page.) Please go to the Waterloo Inn's web site to register until December 10, 2003.

     Double Occupancy $118.00 CAD
     Suites from $199.00 CAD
