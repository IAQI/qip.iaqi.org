Sponsorship

QIP 2026 presents a unique opportunity to build on the momentum of the International Year of Quantum (IYQ), fostering long-term impact and sustained public engagement in the rapidly evolving field of quantum technologies. 

As the premier annual conference in quantum information science, QIP offers sponsors unparalleled visibility and industry recognition. Partnering with QIP 2026 enables organizations to: 

  • Showcase leadership in quantum innovation
  • Engage with top-tier researchers, thought leaders, and emerging talent
  • Establish strategic collaborations and explore new business opportunities 

We invite companies, research institutions, and public organizations to join us in shaping the future of quantum technologies.

For more information about sponsorship opportunities, please contact the Local Organizing Team at qip2026@lu.lv.
