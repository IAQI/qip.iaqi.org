Uploading Page



This page can be used by the invited speakers to upload their abstract. Just fill the following fields, if you can provide a full paper then fill the appropriate section. Otherwise just leave it empty. If you want to modify an existing entry you should fill all fields again (from scratch) and send me an e-mail describing which entry you would like us to remove. If you experienced problems with this web page send me an e-mail.

Once you have uploaded your abstract, you can verify if it has been included correctly in the database by following the link (to your entry) appearing in the information page. However, do not forget to press the reload button of your browser in order to get the updated page.

If there is less than four authors fill the author fields from the first to the last one. Do not start filling in the middle. If there is more than four authors then fill the four athor fields below and send us an e-mail with the name of the missing authors.

You can put HTML tags in the abstract description. Tags are then interpreted normally and will be normally interpreted in the abstract list page.

In order to upload your abstract you must know the password. If you did not receive it write to us and we will send it to you.
