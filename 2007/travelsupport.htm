Information about travel support

A limited amount of travel support for international students and postdocs may be available, subject to budgetary constraints. We anticipate being able to pay each person supported approximately AU$1500.

If you would otherwise be unable to attend, and wish to be considered for this support, please email qip@physics.uq.edu.au to register your interest, prior to November 3. Please attach a one page CV, plus a list of your publications.
