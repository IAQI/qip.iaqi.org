PHD STIPENDS:

This section is for PhD students who want to apply for a stipend covering registration fee, accommodation and living expenses. It does not cover the traveling expense. The number of stipends available is limited. The deadline to apply for such a stipend is December 15. The conditions are:

  • You must be a PhD student,
  • Your supervisor must acknowledge that you are a PhD student. This can be done by sending an e-mail to aqip@brics.dk.
