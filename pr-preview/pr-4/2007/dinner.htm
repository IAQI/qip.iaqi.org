Conference Dinner

The Conference Dinner will be held at Palazzo Versace, Gold Coast

on Thursday 1 February 2007 at 7pm.

Your registration fee includes the cost of dinner, drinks and transport. However, if you would like to bring a guest/s (at a cost of $130 per head including transport, dinner, drinks and GST), please indicate the number of extra dinner tickets you require when you register.

Transport

Coach transport will depart from outside The Conservatorium Southbank, at 5.30pm. The return coach will depart Palazzo Versace at 10pm (arriving Southbank at approximately 10.45pm.

Alternatively, travel by train from South Brisbane to Helensvale (approximate cost $10). From Helensvale take a taxi to Palazzo Versace (approximate cost $40). For train information timetable click here.

If you miss the return bus

The last train from Helensvale to South Brisbane departs Helensvale at 10.58pm. Travel to Helensvale station (by taxi) from Palazzo Versace will take approximately 40 minutes.
